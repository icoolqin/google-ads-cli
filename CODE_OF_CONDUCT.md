# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect other users' credentials, account data, and privacy.

Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, sexualized language or attention, publishing another
person's private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered
inappropriate are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for behavior they judge to be inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using the contact method described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially
as practical. Maintainers who are the subject of a report must recuse themselves from its
handling.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
